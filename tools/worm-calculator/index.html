How many worms do I need?

A common rule of thumb in vermicomposting: worms can eat roughly half their body weight in food scraps per day. Tell us about your household and we'll estimate a sensible starter quantity — plus the bin size to house them.

Heads up: this is a starting estimate, not a lab measurement. Start on the lower end — a healthy bin's worm population grows to match the food supply within a few months. Read how many worms do I need for the full explanation.
