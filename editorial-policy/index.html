Editorial policy

Research

  • We base guidance on university extension publications, peer-reviewed composting literature where relevant, and long-documented practitioner experience.
  • We do not invent statistics, studies, or expert quotes. When evidence is thin, we say so.
  • Hands-on claims ("we tested") appear only where genuine testing happened.

Writing

  • Every article targets one clear reader question and answers it completely.
  • No keyword stuffing, no filler sections, no duplicated content across pages.
  • We write for apartment dwellers first — small-space constraints shape every guide.

Review & updates

  • Articles carry published and updated dates. We review troubleshooting and how-to content at least yearly.
  • Corrections are fixed promptly and the update date is refreshed.

Independence

  • We don't sell worms or bins. If we ever use affiliate links, they'll be clearly labeled — and they'll never change what we recommend.
